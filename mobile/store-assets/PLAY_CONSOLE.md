## Store listing (Grow → Store presence → Main store listing)

**App name** (30 max): `MyCoach / MyCaddie`

**Short description** (80 max, this is 73):

```
Your AI golf coach, 90-day training plan and on-course caddie in one app.
```

**Full description** (4000 max):

```
MyCoach / MyCaddie from Dominus Golf puts a personal coach in your pocket for practice, and a caddie at your side on the course.

YOUR 90-DAY PROGRAM
Work through six phases, from putting and short game to irons, woods and driver. Every day brings a session of drills matched to your level, with weekly skills tests to show you are ready to move on.

A COACH WHO KNOWS YOUR GAME
Pick from four coaches, each with their own style. Ask anything - why you slice, what to practise today, how to stop three-putting. Your coach can see your rounds, practice sessions, shots and swing notes, so the advice is about your game, not golf in general.

SWING ANALYSIS
Record your swing or choose a video from your phone. The app reads frames through the swing and tells you what you are doing well, what to work on, and which drills will help.

AN ON-COURSE CADDIE
Choose your course and tee, and get a club recommendation for every shot that accounts for distance, wind, elevation, lie and your own club distances. Ask your caddie a question by typing or by voice.

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
- Hole-by-hole scoring with putts, fairways and greens in regulation
- A handicap index calculated to World Handicap System rules
- Stats and trends across your rounds
- Launch monitor sessions: log carry, ball speed, smash factor and spin by club
- My Bag: your real carry distance for every club
- Practice streaks to keep you consistent

VOICE WHEN YOU WANT IT
Dictate questions instead of typing, and tap "Hear it" to have any answer read aloud. Voice is always optional - nothing plays unless you ask.

Advice in the app is generated by AI and can be wrong. Use your own judgement, and use Report on anything that looks harmful or misleading.
```

## First release (Test and release → Testing → Internal testing)

1. **Testers** tab → Create email list → add your address and your boss's.
2. **Create new release** → upload the `.aab` from the next
   `eas build -p android --profile production`. **Not version code 6** - that
   build cannot open the gallery on Android 12 and below. The first upload
   must be by hand; after that `eas submit` can do it.
3. Release name: `1.0.0 (<version code>)`. Release notes:
   ```
   First beta. Please try every tab and report anything that looks wrong.
   ```
4. Save → Review release → Start rollout to Internal testing.
5. Copy the **opt-in link** from the Testers tab and open it on each tester's
   device (or in BlueStacks). **Uninstall the sideloaded APK first** — Play
   signs with a different key, so it cannot update over it.
